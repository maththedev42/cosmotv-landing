E06Before you ask

Questions people ask before the premiere

When does CosmoTV launch?

There's no date yet. CosmoTV will premiere on iPhone, iPad, Android and the web. Leave your email and we'll tell you as soon as you can install it.

Can I bring my TV Time history?

Yes. CosmoTV imports your TV Time history from a CSV or JSONL file and matches the titles to rebuild your library.

Do I need to create an account?

No. Without an account, your library stays on your device. Syncing across devices is optional and part of CosmoTV Pro.

How much is CosmoTV Pro?

R$8.90 a month or R$89.90 a year, auto-renewing. The yearly plan is 15% cheaper than twelve months of the monthly one. Following shows, marking episodes, importing, exporting and reminders stay free.

Can I take my data with me?

Any time: export your library to CSV, JSON, PDF or Excel. If you have an account, you can also ask us to delete it and its data.

Delete your account →
Do the AI suggestions send my library to a server?

No. The Pro suggestions are generated on your own device, and your library isn't sent to outside AI services.
